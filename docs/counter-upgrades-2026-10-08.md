# Optional counter upgrades

Corner → Decorate now offers Classic / La Marzocco espresso machines and Classic / Fellow electric kettles. The Pothos is an additional movable decoration. Classic equipment and the original six decorations remain the defaults; older saved rooms migrate to those defaults without changing their finishes.

The self-contained GLBs were selected from the supplied exports. Duplicate ZIPs and the larger machine variant are unnecessary. Models are lazy loaded, cached, normalized to café coordinates, shadow enabled, and retained in custom photographs. Failed downloads keep the procedural equipment visible and allow retrying the choice. Credits and CC BY 4.0 source links appear in Decorate and public/models/README.md.

The Fellow kettle body lifts during pour-over while its electrical base stays parked. Shot cups and extraction streams shift onto the La Marzocco tray; steaming moves the jug and steam origin to its right side. Selecting classic equipment restores the original placements.

Validation: TypeScript and production build passed. Nine room/workshop/direction tests passed, including migration, optional defaults, upgrade and pothos persistence, and invalid selection rejection. Browser checks covered model previews, installed equipment, the Fellow pour-over, espresso completion, and a successful 1080 × 1080 custom photo preview.
